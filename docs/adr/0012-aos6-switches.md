# AOS 6 Switches are lab Switches, driven through their CLI, with no AOS release install

Titouan asked for AOS 6 Switches to be onboarded as normal lab Switches (#108, 2026-11-04). The lab's first one is 10.69.144.148, an OS6450-48 on 6.7.2.28.R06. Everything a lab Switch goes through applies: onboarding and Contributions, Reservations and Switch accounts, Inspections and Cleanups, the Sweep, Snapshots and Restores. One thing does not: BLab never installs an AOS release on them. AOS 6's last release is years old and every AOS 6 Switch runs it (Titouan). Everything below was checked on .148, where Titouan allowed any command, reloads included.

## Decisions

- **One code path per family, chosen by the session.** `LabSwitch` keeps one method per step. Its session says whether it speaks AOS 6 (`Session.aos6`), and where AOS 6 differs the method takes AOS 6's commands and files. The session is chosen when BLab connects, by the SSH server's version string: `SSH-2.0-OpenSSH_5.0` on AOS 6, `SSH-2.0-OpenSSH-FIPS(capable)` on every AOS 8 Switch read (.132, .139, .140). The version string only settles 6.7.2 R06: R08 (.186, .187) says `SSH-2.0-OpenSSH_9.6-FIPS(capable)`, as AOS 8 does on .131, .136 and .152. So the model settles it first, before BLab connects: the Switch's record, or the model a Contribution under way names (#129). AOS 6 is discontinued, so `clean_state.AOS6_MODELS` is a closed list: every OS6x50 and its kin. What a Switch should hold (its Golden init, its Baseline) is decided by its model instead (`clean_state.runs_aos6`: OS6450 and its AOS 6 kin).
- **AOS 6's CLI is driven as a person drives it (`ShellSession`).** AOS 6's SSH server takes no exec request: it treats one as SCP (`SCP: Invalid command received show` in its log), and every AOS 8 step is an exec. So BLab opens one interactive shell and learns its prompt at login (`-> `, or the Golden init's `OS6450-48-> `). It then sends each command and reads until the prompt comes back. An `ERROR` line in the output is a failure. Each step is bounded as on AOS 8.
- **Files go over SFTP, on a second connection.** AOS 6 opens one channel per SSH connection: a second one, inside the shell's connection, is "Administratively prohibited". So the session opens a second connection the first time it needs a file, and lists directories over it too. AOS 6's `ls -a` is refused, and `ls` of a missing directory lists `/flash` instead. SFTP starts in `/flash`, as on AOS 8, so paths are the same relative ones.
- **BLab stays under AOS 6's SSH rate.** AOS 6 takes 3 new SSH sessions a minute. A 4th counts as an attack, and for the next minute it takes only one, until 3 quiet minutes have passed (Switch Management Guide 6.7.2, p. 2-18). Over that, it closes each new connection before its banner. A Switch worker look at a reloading Switch alone takes two steps every 30 s. So:
  - BLab keeps each AOS 6 Switch's CLI session open between steps, for 45 s of quiet (`AOS6_IDLE_CLOSE`), and reuses it. One step uses it at a time.
  - Each process opens at most 2 new connections a minute to one AOS 6 Switch.
  - A connection closed before its banner all the same (another process, the other stage) is retried after 65 s, 4 times in all. A recorded Switch is known as AOS 6 by its model before BLab first connects to it. An address BLab knows nothing of (onboarding) is tried once: an AOS 8 Switch still booting would otherwise hold its caller a minute.
  - Pre-prod and production both drive the lab (ADR 0002), each with its own count, so the retry is what absorbs the other.
- **The Golden init carries the management.** AOS 6 has no vcsetup.cfg: it boots `working/boot.cfg` and the images beside it, and nothing else. The OS6450 has no EMP: its management is an IP interface on VLAN 1443, untagged on its last copper port (2/48 on .148). That is its Management port, as CONTEXT.md defines it. So AOS 6's Golden init is a `boot.cfg` in AOS 6's words with that management in it, the lab's routes, LLDP TLVs, and `aaa accounting session "local"` (below). BLab makes the Switch an `init/` that holds only that file. AOS 6 ignores `init/`. Onboarding finds the Management port in the running config: the port untagged in the VLAN that holds the Switch's address. A Switch whose config doesn't say is not written to.
- **The Cleanup copies `init/boot.cfg` into `working/` and reloads.** It first removes the Switch accounts and Unwanted files, as on AOS 8. Then it puts back from `certified/` any image of `working/` that isn't the size of `certified/`'s. `certified/` holds the lab's release, which nothing changes on AOS 6. Once the Switch is back clean, `copy working certified` certifies it (#99). That command needs no confirmation and only copies what differs.
- **No release is installed.** Neither the lab release, nor a member's AOS install, nor a Restore's. tsbuddy's family table (ADR 0011) doesn't know AOS 6 models, so they have no lab release and no compatible release. `install_images` refuses an AOS 6 Switch all the same. A Snapshot taken on an AOS 6 Switch holds `working/boot.cfg` and no VC setup. A Restore writes it back into `working/` and reloads on the release the Switch runs.
- **The Sweep reads logins from AOS 6's log.** AOS 6 logs who logs in only with session accounting on, so the Golden init turns it on. Its log files are binary, and AOS 6's CLI has no `grep`. BLab reads `show log swlog appid aaa`, which prints the `AAA login,<user>,<SSH|TELNET>,,session=…,<address>` lines in a second. The log outlives reboots, so "since the last reload" is a window by the Switch's own clock: from `show system`'s Date & Time less its uptime, to now.
- **Switch accounts in AOS 6's words.** `user <name> password "<password>" read-write all` (or `read-only all`). AOS 6 has no root shell and refuses `su`. Its CLI takes double quotes as quotes and a single quote as part of the password (checked by logging in). ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_REFUSED keeps both kinds of quote out of passwords. No `write memory` follows: AOS 6 keeps its local users in their own table at once. A user made without it was still there after a reload on .148. `write memory` is also refused while the Switch runs from `certified/`, and would write a member's running config into `working/boot.cfg`.
- **Ports are slot/port.** AOS 6 names ports `2/48`, the chassis being its stack slot (`show chassis`: `Chassis 2`). BLab records them as AOS 6 names them. The parsers take both forms: `show interfaces`, `show interfaces capability`, `show lldp remote-system` (`Agents on Local Slot/Port 2/48`, its Port Description before its System Name) and the chassis IDs. A front port, though, is the Switch's AOS's only (#113, `clean_state.front_panel_port`, by its model): `slot/port` on AOS 6, `chassis/slot/port` on AOS 8. So Inspection's Unwanted cables, Contribute a cable, onboarding's LLDP Ports and port capabilities take no `2/48` on an AOS 8 Switch, and no `1/1/48` on an AOS 6 one. A Backbone runs AOS 8.
- **Onboarding gives an AOS 6 Switch slot 1** (#113). A standalone OS6450 may come in stack slot 2, its ports `2/x` (the lab's first one did). Before its Golden init is installed, onboarding renames its Management port `1/<port>` in `working/boot.cfg` (the reload would cut it off otherwise), runs `stack set slot <n> saved-slot 1 reload` (it asks `Confirm set slot operation with reload (Y/N)?`), and goes on once it is back, its ports `1/x`. Its Onboarding row stays Recorded meanwhile, `reload_started_at` saying since when, so no new step is needed and production's older code reads it. A Switch in slot 1 already is left alone. One whose `working/boot.cfg` doesn't name its Management port isn't touched. Done by hand on the OS6450 at 10.69.144.175 on 2026-11-04.

## Considered options

- **Ask the database which family a Switch is before connecting.** First rejected: onboarding meets Switches no record names, and the SSH version string was free. Taken since #129, as the version no longer tells R08 from AOS 8. A Contributed Switch's model is known from its Contribution. An address nothing names (`onboard_switches --ips`) still goes by its version, so an R08 Switch is onboarded through Contribute a Switch.
- **Probe an unknown address with an exec, which AOS 6 drops (#129).** Rejected: AOS 6 is discontinued, so its models are a closed list, and every lab Switch BLab meets has a record or a Contribution.
- **Telnet, which AOS 6 also answers.** Rejected: cleartext passwords, and AOS 8 Switches would still need SSH.
- **Keep `init/`'s images too, as on AOS 8.** Rejected: an OS6450 has 53 MB free and its images take 28 MB, and BLab never changes its release, so `certified/` serves as their copy.
- **Translate the AOS 8 Golden init.** Rejected: too few lines are shared. AOS 6's is its own, booted on .148 and read back line by line.

## Consequences

- An AOS 6 step costs at most two SSH sessions (the CLI and SFTP), often none when the session is kept. A step can wait up to a minute for the Switch's rate. A Cleanup or onboarding is slower by that much, never refused for it.
- `show configuration snapshot` on AOS 6 prints defaults the Golden init doesn't say (`ip service all`, `bridge mode 1x1`…), adds `ifindex 1` to IP interfaces and doubles some spaces. The comparison of the running config with `init/` ignores those (`inspection.AOS_DEFAULT_LINES`, IFINDEX, SPACES).
- Restoring onto a replacement Switch (#46) offers no AOS 6 Switch, and never one of the other AOS. The config rewrite knows only chassis/slot/port, and AOS 6 and AOS 8 configs don't translate.
- A Contribution takes an AOS 6 Switch only without an EMP, as the OS6450 is. Its console commands are AOS 6's.
